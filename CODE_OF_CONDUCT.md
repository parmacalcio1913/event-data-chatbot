# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

Contributors, maintainers, and anyone participating in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, manual testing channels) are expected to follow it.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers at **analytics@parmacalcio1913.com**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows the four-tier "Community Impact Guidelines" defined in section 4 of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, ranging from a private warning through to a permanent ban for sustained or severe violations.
